Privacy

Workouts · last updated 25 September 2026

Workouts is a personal gym log. It has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Nobody but you can see what you log in it.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter — workouts, sets, weights, plans, routines and your name — is stored in your browser on the device you use. It does not leave that device unless you turn on Google Drive sync.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to sync, you sign in with Google and the app saves one file, workout-tracker-data.json, to your own Google Drive, and reads it back to keep your devices in step.

  • The app asks only for Google's drive.file permission. That lets it see and change the files it created itself — nothing else in your Drive.
  • The file goes directly between your device and Google. It never passes through any server belonging to this app.
  • You can stop syncing at any time, and delete the file from your Drive like any other file. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Workouts's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is used only to provide sync to you. It is not shared, sold, used for advertising, or read by anyone.

Other services

When you use sync, your browser loads Google's sign-in script from accounts.google.com. The “how to perform this” links open a YouTube search in your browser when you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy policies. The app itself is served by GitHub Pages.

Cookies and tracking

The app sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics of any kind.

Deleting your data

Clearing the site's data in your browser, or deleting the app from your home screen, removes everything stored on that device. If you used sync, delete workout-tracker-data.json from your Google Drive as well.
